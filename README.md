YU SciFi is a reference catalog of the science fiction and fantasy books published in Yugoslavia during the 20th century — 510 titles across 17 book series.

• Kentaur (1967–1968, 1976–1989) • Polaris (1982–2001) • Znak Sagite (1985–1999) • X-100 SF (1985–1988) • And the rest: Zvezdane Staze, Zoroaster, Supernova, Rune, Stilos, NF Strugacki i Legvin, A-Z Biblioteka, Mali Polaris, Plava ptica, Raspust, plus separately published titles and a shelf of criticism and essays

Search translated and original metadata and see full screen cover art. Each book links to Google, Goodreads (bilingual), and Wikipedia, and also to the marketplaces and auction sites where these books still can be bought: Limundo / Kupindo (Serbia), Njuškalo (Croatia), Bolha in Slovenia, Olx/Pik (Bosnia and Herzegovina).

The whole catalog ships inside the app. No account, no sign-in, and no network connection needed to browse it. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking, and nothing collected about you.
